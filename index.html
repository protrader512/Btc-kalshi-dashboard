Model information

This calculator estimates the probability of BTC finishing above or below the contract target at expiration using the current BTC price, remaining time and an assumed hourly volatility. It is a mathematical decision-support model, not a guarantee of the result. BTC can move suddenly, and the actual contract settlement rules, fees, spread and market liquidity can affect the outcome.

Educational decision-support tool. It does not connect to your Kalshi account or place trades automatically.
